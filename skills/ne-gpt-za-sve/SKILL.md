# Bane icon-card edit

Respect the user's latest instructions and chosen editor. This style can be built in Remotion or HyperFrames; follow the relevant framework skill for implementation and validation. The [approved card example](references/icon-card-example.png) shows the visual convention.

## Picture and text

- If the speaker has already prepared the cuts, keep the supplied footage and its voice continuous. Add the requested graphics and audio on separate layers. Change cuts only when requested.
- Time each category and icon to the spoken phrase. Use short yellow cards with bold black category text. Anchor each card's **bottom edge** about 5% of the video height above the top of the speaker's head; this keeps one-line and two-line cards at the same clearance. Check several frames because the head moves.
- Every standalone brand icon or logo overlay uses the same white rounded square card: black uppercase name centered at the top, colored icon below, centered and uncropped. Do not put a loose logo or a separate floating name beside it. This applies to the hook icon too unless the user requests another treatment.
- Use bold white text with a dark outline for spoken emphasis. Animate a short hook word by word or line by line, then keep it readable. Keep all text clear of the face and mouth. If the hook names a tool, spell the tool name as text and place its icon card beneath the complete phrase.
- Position cards around the actual framing and gestures rather than copying pixel coordinates from another shot. Keep a safe margin around the frame edges.

## Sound and delivery

- Preserve intelligible speech. When a music track is provided, mix it quietly underneath the voice and check the relationship at the loudest part of the track. Do not assume a gain value from another song transfers unchanged.
- Put one short, discreet click at the entrance of each icon card, including a hook icon. Keep the clicks at a consistent level; percentage requests change the current SFX gain proportionally.
- Preview the hook, a short category, a wrapped category, and the ending. Verify icon appearance and click timing. Leave an editable project and preview when the user plans to export manually; export only when requested.
